## Scope

Landing page (Persuade) plus the signed-in workspace (Operate) of Analyst Buddy. Visitor: a non-technical business owner. Action: request access (email form, working on the page); existing customers sign in from the nav.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every week starts at the board: what the numbers did, why, and who acts. Refuses the category default of a dark gradient hero beside a dashboard screenshot with a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: White dry-erase enamel ground with faint ghosted erasures and a ruled grid; brushed aluminium frame and marker tray as the structural chrome; ink in four marker colors (black #1B1E23 for text, blue #1F4FD8 for action, red #D7262E only for misses, green #128A4B only for hits); magnets (yellow #FFC93C) pin cards and printouts; marker hand reserved for numbers and annotations, all copy in a sturdy grotesk. Raised: every figure carries a small source tag (rows read, code run) [provenance]; the ruled lines are the layout grid [ascii]; the one number that matters is five times larger [tropicalia]; insights ranked most urgent first [bazaar]; copy in two-line notes [coffeehouse].

STORY: The owner sees a real weekly review on a board, understands the analyst found what changed and why from their own data, believes the numbers because each shows its source, and pins their email to get access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width board in an aluminium frame filling the viewport under a slim nav (Sign in at right). Left two thirds: marker headline, a huge red "-12%" with source tag, three ranked notes. Right third: a yellow-magnet card with the working request access form. Marker tray along the bottom edge.

FORM: The Monday Board, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 926ab91e. Signature interaction: figures and notes write themselves on in marker strokes on load; the eraser wipes and rewrites when the sample week changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
